# Ain Shams University Medicine Curriculum Structure

This document outlines the standard 5-year modular curriculum at Ain Shams University Faculty of Medicine (FOM-ASU) used to organize study materials.

---

## 1. Academic Structure Overview

The medical program spans 5 years divided into two primary phases:
1. **Pre-Clerkship / Integrated Phase (Years 1–3)**: System-based integrated modules covering basic medical sciences (Anatomy, Histology, Physiology, Biochemistry, Pathology, Pharmacology, Microbiology, Parasitology) along with clinical skills and community medicine.
2. **Clerkship / Clinical Phase (Years 4–5)**: Rotations through clinical specialties (Internal Medicine, General Surgery, Pediatrics, Obstetrics & Gynecology, and subspecialties).

## 3. Full Faculty Curriculum Mapping (Reference)

### Year 1: Foundation & Early Systems
- **Foundation Module (MED101)**
- **Musculoskeletal System (MED102)**
- **Cardiovascular System 1 (MED103)**

### Year 2: Major Organ Systems
- **Blood & Lymphatic System (MED201 - Active)**
- **Respiratory System (MED202)**
- **Gastrointestinal System & Nutrition (MED203)**
- **Endocrine & Metabolism (MED204)**
- **Renal & Urinary System (MED205)**

### Year 3: Neurosciences, Reproduction & Pre-clinical Integration
- **Central Nervous System (MED301)**: Neuroanatomy, neurophysiology, neuropathology, neuropharmacology.
- **Special Senses (MED302)**: Eye, ear, nose, throat fundamentals.
- **Reproductive System (MED303)**: Male and female reproductive systems, embryology, genetics.
- **Integumentary & Multi-system Integration (MED304)**: Dermatology fundamentals, systemic pathology, transition to clinical practice.

### Year 4: Major Clinical Specialties (Part 1)
- **General Surgery**: Upper GI, lower GI, vascular, breast, endocrine surgery, emergency surgery.
- **Internal Medicine (Part 1)**: Cardiology, pulmonology, gastroenterology, nephrology.
- **Pediatrics**: Neonatology, growth & development, pediatric infections, pediatric nutrition.
- **Community & Occupational Medicine**: Epidemiology, biostatistics, public health, preventive medicine.

### Year 5: Major Clinical Specialties (Part 2) & Subspecialties
- **Obstetrics & Gynecology**: Normal & high-risk pregnancy, labor, gynecologic oncology, reproductive endocrinology.
- **Internal Medicine (Part 2) & Subspecialties**: Neurology, rheumatology, hematology, oncology, infectious diseases.
- **Specialized Surgery**: Orthopedics, ophthalmology, ENT, neurosurgery, urology.
- **Forensic Medicine & Clinical Toxicology**: Medical ethics, legal medicine, toxic exposure management.

## 3. General & Cross-Year Resource Categories

In addition to modular resources, students require cross-cutting materials:
- **General Drives**: Comprehensive batch drives containing multi-year archives.
- **Student Activity & Channels**: Official ASU batch channels, exam coordination, student union announcements.
- **Medical Skills & USMLE / IFOM Prep**: Question banks, First Aid references, clinical examination video guides.
